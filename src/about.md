## A bit more about me

I'm Clark Ymata, currently a Grade 12 student taking Senior High School in
Quezon City, Philippines. I'm studying at Holy Spirit National High School,
where I'm working through my final year before graduation — balancing
requirements, projects, and (hopefully) a bit of free time on the side.

I was born on February 27, and like a lot of people from Quezon City, I grew
up with adobo as a constant on the table — it's easily my favorite food,
whether it's made with chicken, pork, or both. If you ask me to pick a color
for anything, I'll pick blue almost every time.

This blog is where I plan to write about what I'm learning in Senior High
School, things I'm interested in outside of class, and whatever else is worth
putting into words. More posts coming soon.
